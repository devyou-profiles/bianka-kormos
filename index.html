About

I believe creativity can be a way of learning, communicating and connecting with other people.

I finished high school in Pécs in 2024 and soon after moved to Menorca, Spain, for a year with the European Solidarity Corps. Working with children there showed me how much I enjoy creating spaces where people can learn and express themselves.

Performing has always been part of who I am. I have been dancing hip-hop for around five years and spent two years in a student theatre group. For me, education and the performing arts are not separate worlds. Both are about communication, confidence and people.

RomaniaLacu Roșu12–21 October 2026

“ReForest”

Reforestation, biodiversity & climate action

An Erasmus+ youth exchange hosted by the AGOJE Association at a mountain lodge by Lacu Roșu, in the Eastern Carpathians. It brought together 40 young people and 8 group leaders from Romania, Portugal, the Czech Republic and Hungary for ten days about forests: why they disappear, how digital tools can help protect them, and how we can act ourselves.

  • Workshops on deforestation, biodiversity and climate action, with simulations and role play
  • Digital tools for nature: iNaturalist, Pl@ntNet and our own digital footprint
  • Tree species, planting basics and drone mapping to design the planting site
  • A collective tree-planting action, documented together
  • Team building, a city visit and intercultural evenings
  • Daily reflection, final presentations, a ReForest blog and a Youthpass certificate

GreeceElafonisos23 September – 3 October 2026

“Y-Cycle”

Digital & AI skills, creativity, entrepreneurship

An Erasmus+ youth exchange that brought together 37 young people from Greece, Portugal, Hungary and Romania on the island of Elafonisos. Hosted by Community Cohorts in Greece, it was ten days of hands-on learning about how technology and creativity can turn ideas into real projects.

  • Prompt engineering: how to talk to AI tools and get useful results
  • Recording a podcast and making music with digital tools
  • Designing and building a game with AI and Visual Studio Code
  • Building my own website and Europass CV
  • Entrepreneurship, automation and CRM systems as a digital assistant
  • Ethics, online safety and the responsible use of AI
  • Pitching my ideas in front of the whole group
  • Teamwork, intercultural evenings and daily reflection, with a Youthpass certificate

What I took homeDigital and AI skills I can use straight away, the confidence to present my ideas in front of everyone, and a clear picture of how an idea becomes a real project. This website is one of them.

CroatiaOrahovica9–16 August 2026

“Discover Yourself”

Mental health, self-discovery & emotional resilience

An Erasmus+ youth exchange hosted by EuroActive Hrvatska that brought together 25 young people and 5 youth leaders from Croatia, Hungary, Italy, Slovakia and Spain. In a safe and supportive environment, we learned from each other how our emotions and thoughts work, and built skills for psychological resilience.

  • Workshops on primary and secondary emotions, thoughts and personal values
  • Discovering our character strengths, social circles and protective factors
  • Practical tools for problem-solving, conflict management and relaxation
  • Creative expression, with every national team running its own workshop
  • Group building, an intercultural night, a board game night and our own Eurovision
  • Daily reflection groups and a Youthpass certificate
  • Green travel: we arrived by train, not by plane

What I took homeMore self-awareness, calmer ways of handling stress, and the confidence to talk openly about feelings, which I now bring into my work with children.
